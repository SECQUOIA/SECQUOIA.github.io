SECQUOIA's teaching goal is to prepare students to become critical thinkers, independent learners, and effective collaborators who can model, analyze, and improve complex process systems. In an era of abundant data and rapid technological change, the ability to evaluate and apply mathematical and computational methods is crucial for sound engineering decisions in industry and society. We design structured, engaging courses that emphasize active learning, real-world process applications, and team-based problem solving, treating teaching and mentoring as opportunities to connect theory with practice.

## Teaching Philosophy and Guiding Principles

Our teaching philosophy emphasizes active engagement, clarity of expectations, and alignment between learning outcomes and assessments. Instruction is organized around four guiding principles:

1. **Clear structure and alignment** — Lectures, assignments, and assessments map directly to explicit learning outcomes and incorporate low-stakes checks and timely feedback to monitor student progress.
2. **Application-driven, project-based learning** — Coursework ties mathematical formulations and computational tools to real process systems through engineering case studies and semester-long team projects.
3. **Flexibility for diverse backgrounds** — Course materials include modular refreshers and targeted exercises for varied preparation levels alongside advanced open-ended challenges.
4. **Teamwork as a core engineering skill** — Classroom projects mirror real-world interdisciplinary collaboration, developing technical communication, leadership, and reproducible project workflows.

## Classroom Experience and Curriculum

Group instructors and trainees bring instructional experience spanning chemical engineering, mathematics, physics, operations research, and computer science—from core undergraduate courses in process dynamics and control to interdisciplinary graduate electives in data science, discrete optimization, and quantum computing. Explore current and past offerings on our [Courses page](/teaching/courses.html).

## Mentoring and Student Outcomes

Mentoring is integral to SECQUOIA's pedagogy. Faculty and senior researchers mentor postdoctoral fellows, doctoral and master's students, and undergraduate scholars across engineering, physics, computer science, and operations research. Structured mentoring and professional development have supported trainees in earning competitive fellowships, presentation awards, internships, open-source software contributions, and research placements.

## Connection to Research and Open Education

Teaching in SECQUOIA is tightly coupled with our research and open-science commitments. Courses incorporate contemporary research benchmarks, emphasize reproducible computational notebooks, and publish lecture code and tutorial materials openly to lower barriers to participation across institutions.

## Continuous Improvement

We continually refine our courses through student feedback, pedagogical training, and collaboration with institutional teaching centers. Inquiries regarding course materials, guest tutorials, or educational collaborations are welcome via the [contact form below](#contact).
