SECQUOIA develops optimization and decision-making methods—spanning classical mathematical programming, quantum and physics-inspired computing, and scientific AI—that transform complex engineering models and data into reliable real-world decisions.

## Mission

- Advance systems-engineering decision-making by creating and disseminating rigorous optimization, machine learning, and artificial intelligence methods as usable open-source software.
- Span classical, hybrid, and quantum computing for high-impact industrial and scientific applications, especially chemical, process, and energy systems.
- Train a diverse and inclusive cohort of engineers and scientists through structured mentorship, teaching, and open, reproducible research practices.

## Vision

Make advanced optimization practical, trustworthy, and broadly accessible—including quantum-enhanced and AI-driven methods where they deliver measurable value—by unifying mathematical theory, scalable algorithms, benchmark libraries, and open tools across emerging computing hardware.
