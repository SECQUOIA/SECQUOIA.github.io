## Opportunities to Join or Collaborate with SECQUOIA

SECQUOIA welcomes inquiries from motivated prospective **Ph.D. students**, **postdoctoral associates**, **visiting scholars**, and **academic, national-laboratory, or industrial collaborators** working at the interface of mathematical optimization, process systems engineering, quantum and emerging computing, and scientific machine learning.

> **Important note on Ph.D. and postdoctoral inquiries:** Prospective Ph.D. students and postdoctoral researchers interested in working with Prof. David E. Bernal Neira should **not** apply through Purdue University admissions for new openings. Instead, please reach out directly through the [Contact & Inquiries form below](#contact) to discuss current opportunities and institutional pathways.

## Who Should Reach Out

- **Prospective Ph.D. Students and Postdoctoral Associates** — Candidates with strong quantitative preparation in chemical engineering, operations research, industrial and systems engineering, computer science, applied mathematics, physics, or closely related disciplines. Experience with mathematical programming (`MILP`, `MINLP`, `GDP`), scientific software development (`Python`/`Pyomo` or `Julia`/`JuMP`), quantum/Ising algorithms, or machine learning for physical and engineering systems is especially valued.
- **Visiting Graduate and Undergraduate Researchers** — Scholars with external fellowships, exchange support, or institutional sponsorship interested in multi-month research visits are encouraged to inquire ahead of their target dates.
- **Academic, National-Laboratory, and Industrial Collaborators** — We actively partner on joint research projects, benchmark problem suites, industrial optimization case studies, guest tutorials, and open-source software development (`MindtPy`, `QUBO.jl`, `GDPLib`, `Pyomo.GDP`).

## How to Inquire

Please send a message to Prof. David E. Bernal Neira using the [Contact & Inquiries form below](#contact) and include:

1. **Intended role and timeline** — Specify whether you are inquiring as a prospective Ph.D. student, postdoctoral fellow, visiting researcher, or collaborator, along with your target start window.
2. **Research alignment** — Briefly highlight one or two [research themes](/5-research.html), [publications](/4-publications.html), or [open-source software tools](/6-software.html) from SECQUOIA that connect with your technical interests and prior work.
3. **CV and scholarly links** — Include links to your CV, Google Scholar, GitHub, ORCID, or academic webpage so we can review your background and code/publications.

## Our Research Culture

Members of SECQUOIA combine rigorous theory, open-source software engineering, and reproducible computational benchmarking in a collaborative, mentorship-focused environment. Learn more about our group on [Mission & Vision](/mission/), [DEI and Outreach](/8-diversity.html), [Members](/2-members.html), and [Principal Investigator](/1-bernalde.html).
